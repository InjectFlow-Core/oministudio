Privacy policy

Last updated 25 September 2026.

This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do with it. The data controller is Injectflow Core, company registration number RC 8860651, of 8 Yellow Duke Street, Calabar, Cross River State, Nigeria, trading as Omini Studio. For questions or requests, write to eze@injectflow.com.

What we collect

  • Account details — your email address, your display name, and how you signed in.
  • Narration recordings — the audio you upload or record in the browser is stored on our servers, along with its transcript. This is voice data, and it is kept until you delete the project or your account.
  • Project content — your brief, the generated plan, drafts, revisions and finished videos.
  • Billing records — what you bought, when, and the credits it granted. Card details never reach our servers; they are handled by our payment partner.
  • Technical logs — request, error and job records used to keep the service working and to investigate faults.

Why we use it

  • To provide the service you asked for: producing, storing and delivering your videos. This is the performance of our contract with you.
  • To take payment and keep the accounting records the law requires.
  • To keep the service secure, diagnose faults and prevent abuse, which is our legitimate interest.
  • To answer your support messages.

We do not sell personal data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use your recordings or videos to train general-purpose AI models.

Who else processes it

We use a small number of service providers under contract: cloud hosting and storage, speech and video processing, email delivery, and payment processing. They act on our instructions and may only use the data to provide their service to us. We are established in Nigeria and some of these providers are elsewhere; where personal data crosses a border, the transfer relies on standard contractual clauses or an equivalent safeguard recognised under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. We will name the current providers on request.

How long we keep it

  • Projects, recordings and videos: until you delete them, or until your account is closed.
  • Account records: for the life of the account, then up to 90 days in backups.
  • Billing records: as long as tax and accounting law requires, normally seven years.
  • Technical logs: up to 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, object to or restrict how we use it, and ask for it in a portable form. Write to eze@injectflow.com and we will reply within 30 days.

We are established in Nigeria and handle personal data under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. If you are unhappy with our answer you may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Cookies

We set a signed cookie to keep you signed in, and store your light or dark theme choice in your browser. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Children

The service is not for people under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you by email or in the app before the change takes effect.
