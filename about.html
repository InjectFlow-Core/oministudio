About

Explainer videos should take an afternoon, not a production team.

What we do

Making an explainer usually means a script, a storyboard, a designer, an animator and a week of revisions. Omini Studio does the middle of that for you and turns your narration into a finished, animated video.

You stay in charge of the result. The draft is there to be changed, nothing is published on your behalf, and the finished video is yours to use commercially once it is paid for.
