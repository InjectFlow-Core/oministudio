About Omini Studio

Calabar, Nigeria.

Omini Studio turns narration audio into a finished, animated explainer video. You upload or record the narration, describe what you want, review the draft, and approve the final.

What we actually do

Making an explainer video normally means a script, a storyboard, a designer, an animator and a week of revisions. Omini Studio does the middle of that automatically: it listens to your narration, plans a scene for every part of what you said, and times the animation to your voice. You stay in charge of the result — the draft is there to be changed, and scoped revisions are part of the flow rather than a favour.

Videos are produced from your own audio. Nothing is published on your behalf, and the finished video is yours to use commercially once it is paid for.
