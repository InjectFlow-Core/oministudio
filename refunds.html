Refund policy

Last updated 22 September 2026.

Omini Studio is a service of Injectflow Core. Orders are handled by our payment partner, Paddle.com Market Ltd, which acts as the merchant of record. Refunds are approved by us and paid back to the original payment method through them.

The short version

Unused credits are refundable for 14 days. Credits already spent on a finished video are not, unless the video failed because of us — in which case you are not charged at all.

Unused credits

Ask within 14 days of the purchase and we will refund any credits from that purchase you have not spent. Partial refunds are fine: if you bought ten credits and spent five, you get five back.

Credits you have spent

A generation that produced a video you could watch and download is a delivered service and is not refundable by default. We still read every request: if the output was unusable for a reason that is ours, we refund the credits or run the work again, whichever you prefer.

When something fails on our side

A generation that fails costs nothing — the credits it reserved are returned to your balance automatically, and no refund is needed. The same applies to re-exporting a video you already approved.

Your statutory right to cancel

If you are a consumer in the UK or the EU, you normally have 14 days to cancel a distance purchase. Because credits give immediate access to a digital service, you agree at checkout that the service begins at once, and the right to cancel is lost for credits you have already spent. Credits you have not spent remain refundable under this policy.

Subscriptions

A subscription can be cancelled at any time and runs to the end of the period you have paid for. We do not refund part of a period once it has started, unless the law where you live says otherwise.

How to ask

Email eze@injectflow.com from the address on your account, with the date of the purchase and what went wrong. We answer within two business days. An approved refund reaches your card or account within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

Chargebacks

Please write to us before asking your bank to reverse a payment. A chargeback suspends the account while it is investigated, and almost everything is quicker to fix directly.
