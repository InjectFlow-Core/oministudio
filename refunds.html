Refund policy

Last updated 25 September 2026.

Omini Studio is a service of Injectflow Core. Orders are handled by our payment partner. Refunds are approved by us and returned to the original payment method through our payment partner.

The short version

All credit purchases are non-refundable. Except as expressly provided in this policy or where required by law, all payments are final. A failed generation costs nothing — the credits it reserved return automatically.

Credits

Credits give immediate access to a digital service, and the purchase is final as soon as payment clears. Except as expressly provided in this policy or where required by law, credits are non-refundable whether or not you have used them.

When something fails on our side

A generation that fails costs nothing — the credits it reserved are returned to your balance automatically, and no refund is needed. The same applies to re-exporting a video you already approved.

Billing errors

If you were charged the wrong amount, charged twice, or charged after cancelling, write to us and we will fix it.

Subscriptions

A subscription can be cancelled at any time and runs to the end of the period you have paid for. We do not refund part of a period once it has started.

How to ask

Email support@oministudio.com from the address on your account, with the date of the purchase and what went wrong. We answer within two business days. The time for an approved refund to reach your card or account depends on the payment method and bank.

Chargebacks

Please write to us before asking your bank to reverse a payment. A chargeback suspends the account while it is investigated, and almost everything is quicker to fix directly.
