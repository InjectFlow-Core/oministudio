The fine print that matters.

  • Credits that expire soonest are spent first, so a plan's allowance goes before a pack.
  • Plan changes take effect next billing period and do not touch credits you hold.
  • Your plan's revision rounds are used before any revision costs credits.
  • Credits have no cash value and cannot move between accounts.
  • Unused credits are refundable for 14 days. See the refund policy.
