What is free

  • Uploading and checking your audio.
  • Viewing, commenting on and downloading work you already own.
  • Retrying anything that failed on our side — a failed generation returns the credits it reserved.
  • Reusing narration you have already approved in another render.

The fine print that matters

  • Plan credits arrive each billing month and expire at the end of it. Bought packs last 12 months.
  • Credits with the earliest expiry are spent first, so a plan's allowance is used before a pack you bought.
  • Changing plan takes effect next billing period and does not rewrite credits you already hold.
  • Credits have no cash value and are not transferable between accounts.
  • Unused credits are refundable for 14 days — see the refund policy.
