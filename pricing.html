How do credits work?

One credit equals one minute of generated video. Partial minutes round up to the next full credit.

How long can each video be?

Max video length is 15 minutes on all plans and credit packs, as long as you have enough credits.

Which credits get spent first?

Credits that expire soonest are used first, so monthly plan credits are spent before purchased credit packs.

Do credits roll over?

Subscription credits reset each billing period. Purchased credit packs are valid for 12 months.

When do plan changes take effect?

At the next billing period. Credits you already hold are not touched.

Can I cash out or transfer credits?

No. Credits have no cash value and cannot move between accounts.

Can I get a refund?

Credit purchases are non-refundable. See the refund policy.

Questions about a plan?

Write to support@oministudio.com and a person will answer.
