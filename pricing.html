Pricing

Prices in US dollars. Tax is added at checkout where it applies.

You pay per video, not per minute of work. One credit is one US dollar. Credits are bought in packs and spent when you generate a video; the price is shown and agreed before anything starts.

What a video costs

Narration lengthGeneration and approved final
Under 10 minutes$5 · 5 credits
10 to 15 minutes$10 · 10 credits
Over 15 minutesNot accepted yet

The price covers the draft and the approved final export. Exporting the final costs nothing extra, and re-exporting an approved video is free.

Revisions

Scoped revisions cost a quarter of a credit per 15 seconds of narration, rounded up, with a minimum of a quarter credit. The cost is quoted before the revision runs. Your plan's included revision rounds are used first.

What is free

  • Uploading and checking your audio.
  • Viewing, commenting on and downloading work you already own.
  • Retrying anything that failed on our side.
  • Reusing narration you have already approved in another render.

Credits

  • Credits are bought in packs from inside the app and appear on your balance as soon as payment is confirmed.
  • Credits bought as a pack do not expire at the end of a billing period.
  • A failed generation returns the credits it reserved.
  • Credits have no cash value and are not transferable between accounts.
